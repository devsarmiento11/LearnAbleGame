WebP decoder: dwebp 1.6.0 (Windows x64)
Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/downloads.webmproject.org/releases/webp/libwebp-1.6.0-windows-x64.zip
Project: https://chromium.googlesource.com/webm/libwebp/
Used to decode a local WebP image into a temporary PNG for the existing image popup.
Android uses its built-in BitmapFactory decoder instead.
